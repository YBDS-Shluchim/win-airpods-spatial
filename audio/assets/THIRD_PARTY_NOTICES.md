## Apple Generic HRTF data

`Apple_Generic_HRTF.bin` is the non-personalized generic HRTF resource shipped inside
`AudioDSP.component` on macOS (consumed by Apple's legacy `HRTFPanner` Audio Unit). It is
Apple's own measured/proprietary data, extracted from a licensed macOS installation for
local interoperability with AirPods-class spatial audio playback. It is not covered by an
open-source license; do not redistribute it outside this project without checking your
own licensing obligations.
